Privacy policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Cartoon Studio is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. This page explains what it does with Google and YouTube data.

What it accesses

When its owner connects a YouTube account, Cartoon Studio asks Google for permission to upload videos to that channel and to read the channel's name (to show which channel it will post to). It does not read anyone else's data, comments, subscribers or viewing history.

How it is used

The permission is used only to upload videos the owner made, to the owner's own channel, with the title, description and visibility the owner chose.

Where it is stored

The sign-in Google gives back is stored only on the owner's computer, in the tool's private settings file. It is never sent anywhere except to Google, to upload videos.

Sharing

Cartoon Studio does not collect, sell or share any personal data with anyone. It has no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions), or with "Disconnect" in the tool. Its use of YouTube follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Open an issue on this site's GitHub page.
